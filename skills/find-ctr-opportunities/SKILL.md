# Find CTR opportunities

Reply in the user's language.

1. `list_sites`, pick the site.
2. `list_suggestions` with `status: pending`.
3. Rank by `impressions` and low `clicks` relative to them (many impressions, few clicks, `position` between 3 and 12 is the sweet spot).
4. Present the top 5 as a short table: page `url`, `focus_query`, `impressions`, `clicks`, `position`, and the one-line `reasoning`.
5. Explain in one sentence why each is a quick win. Do not promise a number of extra clicks: the hub measures it later, on the customer's own data.
6. Offer to apply them with the `audit-and-fix` skill.
